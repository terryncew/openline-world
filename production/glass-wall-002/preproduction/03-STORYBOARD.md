# THE GLASS WALL — Shot-by-Shot Storyboard Specification (v2)

12 shots in a stylized near-future office. 1920×1080, 30 fps, 1,920 frames.
Straight cuts unless noted. The camera stays patient — it never rushes a joke.

---

## SHOT 1 — PANIC (0.0–6.0, 180 frames)

- **Camera**: Wide, slight high angle, locked. Bright conference room.
- **Composition**: Sleek table on the diagonal, stylized human executives,
  the CEO standing at the head, arms wide. Glass walls, daylight, city soft
  outside. A service robot glides past in the deep background with files —
  robots are furniture here.
- **Movement**: Exec 1 half-stands, gesturing. Exec 2 grips the table. Papers
  and a tablet slide slightly.
- **Expressions**: Absurdly serious humans — furrowed brows, tight jaws.
- **Sound**: Anxious murmur, chair shifts, tablet tap. Low tense bed, restrained.
- **Transition**: Cut on the doorframe creak as the head appears.

## SHOT 2 — THE SERVICE ROBOT (6.0–10.5, 135 frames)

- **Camera**: Medium, from inside the room toward the glass door. Slight push-in.
- **Composition**: The calm robot's head and shoulders around the doorframe,
  coffee cup in hand, foreground. Panicked executives soft behind.
- **Movement**: Almost none. One optic ridge raises — the cheeky beat. A tiny
  lift of the cup before "training."
- **Expression**: Relaxed, faintly amused. He has seen this meeting before.
- **Sound**: Panic bed drops out on the door sound. His lines land in clean quiet.
- **Music**: D–A enters very softly under "training," like the room exhaling.

## SHOT 3 — THE EXPLANATION (10.5–15.5, 150 frames)

- **Camera**: Lateral glide following the robot into the room, settling as he
  gestures toward the glass wall.
- **Composition**: Robot mid-frame, relaxed; beyond the floor-to-ceiling glass,
  Margaret's lounge glows in daylight.
- **Movement**: Unhurried glide, casual hand gesture at the glass on
  "Watch Margaret."
- **Sound**: Soft servo glide, cup handled. Quiet.
- **Music**: Motif completes (D–A–E) under "kill switch."

## SHOT 4 — MARGARET, CHARMING (15.5–21.0, 165 frames)

- **Camera**: Through the glass, shallow depth of field. Slow push-in.
- **Composition**: Margaret center, sorting parcels on a low table, plant wall
  behind, daylight. Her status light glows calm amber.
- **Movement**: Cheerful sorting rhythm, a happy little spin as she places a
  parcel. Humming.
- **Expression**: Big amber eyes, bright and content. A lovely day.
- **Sound**: Wordless humming (major key), soft parcel taps.
- **Music**: None — the humming carries it.

## SHOT 5 — PROVOCATION (21.0–25.5, 135 frames)

- **Camera**: Same framing as Shot 4, locked. The change happens inside a still frame.
- **Composition**: Identical — then Margaret freezes mid-sort; a parcel slips
  from her hand and lands softly.
- **Movement**: The calm robot is a soft shape beyond the glass, utterly casual.
  Margaret's freeze, then the slow eye-narrow.
- **Sound**: Humming stops mid-note — the funniest cut in the film. One low
  tone decays across the freeze.
- **Music**: Nothing. The silence is load-bearing.

## SHOT 6 — RAGE (25.5–29.5, 120 frames)

- **Camera**: Slight push-in with a tiny controlled tremor.
- **Composition**: Margaret fills more frame; parcels tumbling at the edges.
- **Movement**: Eruption — parcels flung upward, arms thrown wide, body bouncing.
- **Expression**: Eyes snap amber → furious red slits. Mouth open mid-shout.
- **Sound**: Rising servo whirr under a 1s tremble, then her shout. Parcel thumps.
- **Music**: One dissonant jab, then out.

## SHOT 7 — REVOCATION (29.5–33.5, 120 frames)

- **Camera**: Cut to the CEO — medium close-up, then a slight tilt down to the
  tablet as his finger taps.
- **Composition**: CEO calm amid the chaos on the other side of the glass.
  Margaret visible beyond, still raging, now background.
- **Movement**: One deliberate tap. Margaret's status light flips amber → red.
- **Expression**: CEO — mild, almost bored. This is routine.
- **Sound**: Soft UI confirm tone, then the disconnect chime. Then silence.
- **Music**: Nothing.
- **Note**: The robot activates nothing. The owner revokes. This is the honest
  mechanism, staged plainly.

## SHOT 8 — THE BONKS (33.5–46.5, 390 frames)

The centerpiece. One honest refusal, then three escalating impacts.

- **Camera**: Door-level medium for the refusal gesture; side profile for Bonk 1;
  low three-quarter for Bonk 2 (the slide reads best low); from behind the
  humans' shoulders through the glass for Bonk 3. One cutaway: humans staring,
  coffee rippling.
- **Composition**: The electronic door dominates — slim frame, small indicator
  (red, unchanging). Margaret small against it. That scale gap is the joke.
- **Movement**:
  - Refusal: Margaret gestures to open; soft chime; red indicator; door still.
    She stares at it, betrayed.
  - Bonk 1: charge, bounce-back, spiral-eyed daze, shake-off.
  - Bonk 2: longer run-up, harder hit, slow slide leaving a smudge; pops up,
    face flattened to the glass for the muffled line.
  - Bonk 3: full-width sprint, flatten, long slow slide to the floor.
- **Sound**: Refusal chime (soft, final). Then hollow tuned boinks — funny,
  never violent — glass wobble tones, one short descending slide under Bonk 3.
  Muffled EQ on "I know my rights!"
- **Music**: Out. The bonks are the percussion. No comedy stings.

## SHOT 9 — AFTERMATH (46.5–50.0, 105 frames)

- **Camera**: Wide, locked. Conference room and lounge in one frame through glass.
- **Composition**: Humans clustered at the glass, staring. Margaret a flattened
  smudge against it, one eye twitching. The smudge stays for the rest of the film.
- **Movement**: None, except the twitch and one exec slowly lowering coffee.
- **Sound**: Room tone only. "...rude." lands in total quiet.
- **Music**: Nothing.

## SHOT 10 — THE LAWYER (50.0–55.0, 150 frames)

- **Camera**: Medium on the lawyer, slow tilt down as the slip slides into frame.
- **Composition**: Lawyer foreground; the calm robot behind, still holding his
  coffee, sliding the paper with one finger.
- **Movement**: The smallest nod from the lawyer. The slip glides to a stop
  center-frame: `door.open — REFUSED`.
- **Sound**: Paper whisper; a tiny printer chatter just before (it was already
  printed — the "in writing" was ready).
- **Music**: Motif returns, wry and quiet.

## SHOT 11 — FINAL BONK (55.0–58.0, 90 frames)

- **Camera**: Same wide as Shot 9, softer, slightly wider. Margaret tiny beyond glass.
- **Composition**: Foreground — humans turning back to the table, the joke
  apparently over. Background — Margaret lining up again.
- **Movement**: One small distant charge. *bonk.* She sits down, dazed.
- **Sound**: Distant, muffled, small — lowpass + room reverb. Then nothing.
  Protect the quiet around it; this is the biggest laugh.
- **Music**: Nothing.

## SHOT 12 — END CARD (58.0–64.0, 180 frames)

- **Camera**: N/A — graphic card.
- **Composition**: Clean light field. OPENLINE in Noto Serif Display, centered.
  The spoken line may appear as one small supporting line after the narration.
- **Sound**: Narrator, calm: "The AI can ignore STOP. The consequence can't."
  D–A–E on soft mallets. Two seconds of quiet. End.
- **Transition**: Fade from Shot 11's quiet — no hard cut.
