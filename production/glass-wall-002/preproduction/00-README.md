# THE GLASS WALL — Preproduction Package

Production-ready package for the next OpenLine commercial. Codex handles
technical production; this package is the complete preproduction.

## Contents

1. `01-ASSET-INVENTORY.md` — reusable assets (exact repo paths and commits),
   capture/audio techniques, visual canon, and gaps.
2. `02-SHOOTING-SCRIPT.md` — locked 64-second script (v2). Twelve spoken lines.
3. `03-STORYBOARD.md` — 12 shots with camera, composition, movement,
   expressions, timing, transitions, SFX, and music beats (v2).
4. `04-VISUAL-STYLE-AND-CHARACTERS.md` — v2 art direction: original stylized
   near-future world, animated humans, new character designs, environments,
   receipt prop.
5. `05-SOUND-PLAN.md` — voices, music cues, SFX recipes, mix spec. $0.
6. `06-TECHNICAL-HONESTY.md` — claim-by-claim review and fiction disclosures (v2).
7. `07-CODEX-HANDOFF.md` — asset list, production plan, ship-gate QA criteria,
   flags, and decisions needed.
8. `keyframes/v2-near-future/` — Margaret (charming + furious), calm robot,
   and office environment concept frames. v1 wooden-miniature keyframes
   archived in `keyframes/v1-miniature/` (superseded).

## The film in one breath (v2)

Human executives panic about rogue AI. A cheeky service robot pokes its head
in: "You guys don't use OpenLine? Came across it in training." Through the
glass wall, Margaret the courier robot is having a lovely day sorting parcels —
until the robot casually mentions they're thinking of replacing her. She loses
her mind. The CEO taps his tablet: revoked. Margaret can still move, rage,
and protest, but the protected electronic door rejects her. Bonk. Bonk. Bonk.
The lawyer: "Can we get that in writing?" One final distant bonk.

OPENLINE — "The AI can ignore STOP. The consequence can't."
