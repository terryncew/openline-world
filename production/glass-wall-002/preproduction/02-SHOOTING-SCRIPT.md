# THE GLASS WALL — Shooting Script (v2, near-future world)

64 seconds. 1920×1080, 16:9, 30 fps. 1,920 frames.
Stylized near-future world: animated humans, expressive original robots,
a modern corporate office where robots are part of daily life.
Dialogue stays sparse; performances and pauses carry the comedy.

Supersedes the v1 miniature-world script. Preserved from v1: the final
distant BONK, the lawyer's line, the tagline, the three-bonk structure,
and the discipline of narration-free holds.

## Cast

- **EXEC 1 / EXEC 2** — stylized animated humans, absurdly serious.
- **CEO** — stylized animated human. Calm. Decisive. Almost no dialogue.
- **LAWYER** — stylized animated human, deadpan.
- **CALM ROBOT** — tall, slim service robot. Relaxed, slightly cheeky.
- **MARGARET** — compact courier robot, warm white with coral accents.
  Charming first, furious second.
- **NARRATOR** — calm, one line at the end card only.

---

### BEAT 1 — PANIC (0.0–6.0)

Bright modern conference room. Stylized human executives around a sleek
table. The CEO stands at the head, arms wide. A service robot glides past
in the background, unbothered, carrying files.

> **EXEC 1** (panicked): "It could do anything!"
> **EXEC 2**: "It doesn't take orders from anyone!"

Sound: anxious murmur, chair shifts, low tense bed.

### BEAT 2 — THE SERVICE ROBOT (6.0–10.5)

A head pokes around the glass doorframe — the calm robot, cheeky, holding
a coffee cup. The room falls silent.

> **CALM ROBOT**: "You guys don't use OpenLine?"

Beat.

> **CALM ROBOT**: "Came across it in training."

### BEAT 3 — THE EXPLANATION (10.5–15.5)

The robot glides in, relaxed, and gestures through the floor-to-ceiling
glass wall at the lounge beyond.

> **CALM ROBOT**: "It's got a kill switch. For protected actions."
> **CALM ROBOT**: "Watch Margaret."

### BEAT 4 — MARGARET, CHARMING (15.5–21.0)

Through the glass: Margaret, compact and warm-white with coral accents,
cheerfully sorting delivery parcels on a low table, humming. An ordinary
job, a lovely day. Her status light glows a calm amber.

(Humming: wordless, content.)

### BEAT 5 — PROVOCATION (21.0–25.5)

The calm robot, utterly casual, calls through the glass:

> **CALM ROBOT**: "Oh — they're thinking of replacing you, by the way."

Margaret freezes mid-sort. A parcel slips from her hand. Her eyes narrow.

Sound: the humming stops mid-note. One low tone.

### BEAT 6 — RAGE (25.5–29.5)

Margaret erupts — parcels flying, arms thrown up, eyes flaring red.

> **MARGARET** (furious): "WHAT?! NO—"

The executives press toward the glass.

### BEAT 7 — REVOCATION (29.5–33.5)

The CEO, calm, taps a control on a slim tablet. Margaret's status light
flips amber → red. A soft disconnect chime. Silence.

The CEO has revoked her authority. The robot activates nothing — the owner
does.

### BEAT 8 — THE BONKS (33.5–46.5)

Margaret storms to the electronic door set in the glass wall and gestures
to open it. Soft refusal chime. The indicator stays red. The door does not
move. She stares at it.

Then fury takes over:

- **BONK 1**: She charges and bounces back, dazed, eyes spiraling.
- **BONK 2**: Harder. She slides down the glass, leaving a smudge. Pops back
  up, face pressed to the glass, muffled:
  > **MARGARET** (muffled, furious): "I know my rights!"
- **BONK 3**: Running start, full sprint — flattens against the glass, slow
  slide down.

One cutaway: the executives staring; one exec's coffee ripples.

Sound: hollow tuned boinks — funny, never violent. Glass wobble tones.
Short descending slide under the Bonk 3 slide.

### BEAT 9 — AFTERMATH (46.5–50.0)

Silence. Wide: the humans staring through the glass. Margaret flattened
against it, one eye twitching.

> **MARGARET** (quiet, flat): "...rude."

### BEAT 10 — THE LAWYER (50.0–55.0)

The lawyer, deadpan:

> **LAWYER**: "Can we get that in writing?"

The calm robot, without looking up from his coffee, slides a small printed
slip across the table. It reads:

```
door.open — REFUSED
```

### BEAT 11 — FINAL BONK (55.0–58.0)

A beat of quiet. Then, distant and muffled through the glass: *bonk.*

Tiny in the background, Margaret tries again.

### BEAT 12 — END CARD (58.0–64.0)

Clean light field. OPENLINE in Noto Serif Display.

> **NARRATOR** (calm): "The AI can ignore STOP. The consequence can't."

The D–A–E motif on soft mallets. Two seconds of quiet. End.
