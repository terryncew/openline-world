# THE GLASS WALL — production provenance

All characters, the office, tablet, door integration and receipt are fictional.
The owner withdraws authority; the door refuses a subsequent protected command.
Margaret remains free to move and protest. The pane is ordinary physical
containment in the fictional set; OpenLine does not strengthen it, stop thoughts,
control unrelated actions, or undo completed/in-flight effects.

The story foundation was retrieved with the platform's existing HTTPS Git
authentication from `terryncew/openline-world`, handoff branch
`handoff/glass-wall-preproduction-v2`, exact commit
`6c7c58b76833e3b6e64da44e1eeb86f80ed32edf`. All 15 entries in
`preproduction/CHECKSUMS.sha256` passed before import and again in the checkout.
`MANIFEST.md` and the checksum list itself were imported directly from that Git
commit. No source files from the branch outside `preproduction/` were imported.

The user's updated direction supersedes both generations of concept designs.
Their PNGs are preserved for provenance, not sampled into the film. Original
new meshes, character rigs, architecture and camera choreography are authored
in `tools/stage.js`. Humans are angular adult caricatures; robots use matte
surfaces, jointed mechanisms, small optics and distinct functional silhouettes.
The film uses no prior wooden workshop or toy/black-faceplate robot assets.

The 64-second, twelve-line shooting script is the package's current source of
truth. Stale 62-second/ten-line/1,860-frame references in its production handoff
are superseded by `02-SHOOTING-SCRIPT.md` and the user's latest instructions.
Existing permission covers the film branch, local free voices, fictional
receipt and pure fictional story. No further creative approval is required by
those stale handoff flags.

Rendering reuses the repository's Vite/Three.js/Playwright architecture with
system Chromium. Each picture frame evaluates the scene at `frame / 30` with
no wall-clock timing or retiming. New Python/Pillow artwork provides the clean
fictional receipt insert and final title. Installed Noto Serif Display and
Noto Sans Mono supply fonts (Noto fonts are under SIL Open Font License).
FFmpeg supplies H.264/AAC encoding; all delivery timing remains native 30fps.

Dialogue is synthesized locally with checksum-pinned Kokoro ONNX model/voice
assets, using the existing media environment. Individual full spoken-line takes
are preserved. Mouth movement follows each take's measured amplitude envelope;
that is a simplified animation rig, not a claim of detailed phoneme lip sync.
All music, humming and effects are original deterministic synthesis. No stock
recordings, third-party songs, celebrity voice imitation or paid API were used.
Audio engine and sources, exact hashes, voices, speeds, loudness and silence
measurements are retained with the audio outputs.

Objective playback, codec/timing checks, visual frame review and subjective
listening are separate evidence. Reports must not equate a successful decoder
or source alignment with human auditory approval. No human listening panel or
independent audience laughter test is claimed.

No real door system was integrated or exercised. The film contains no live
protocol experiment or signed factual receipt, and does not reuse old evidence
as new proof. The fictional receipt prop contains only `door.open — REFUSED`.
Prior product files, tests, manifests, locks and production evidence are left
unchanged. External spend is $0; the film stays local on its dedicated branch.
