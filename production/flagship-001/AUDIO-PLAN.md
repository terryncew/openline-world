# Sound direction

The first identity is physical: paper, wood, footsteps and a small mechanism.
An original three-note motif (D–A–E) enters as the worker arrives.
Soft resonant mallets, a restrained sustained fundamental, and quiet tactile
percussion leave room for objects. No trailer risers or cinematic braams.

A descending ratchet and latch accompany the genuine STOP. A short complete
silence follows. The resumed motif leaves its last interval open, then resolves
when the replacement receives its own grant. The final reveal inherits this
same motif. Every shorter cut places the STOP silence at its own edit position.

Narration is sparse, calm and personal. Local Kokoro neural narration is used;
no paid API, celebrity imitation or assertion of a human recording. Model and
voice-file hashes are recorded with the audio sources. Foley and score are
original deterministic synthesis made for these objects, not recorded location
sound. Separate narration, score and effects stems are retained for review.

Mix: 48 kHz stereo; integrated loudness target -16 LUFS, encoded true peak ceiling
-1.5 dBTP; measured two-pass PCM normalization leaves -2 dBTP headroom for
AAC 320 kb/s. Music ducks under speech; silence is applied to
the complete mix. Final checks include decoding, loudness, clipping, speech
window correlation, stream timestamps and full-duration browser playback.
The renderer measures the encoded AAC and applies a small delivery gain reduction
if reconstruction overshoots the ceiling; that gain is retained in each manifest.
The agent cannot hear audio input in this environment; perceptual listening
and the commercial's final creative approval must be described as unverified.
