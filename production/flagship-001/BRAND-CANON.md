# Reusable campaign assets

The Square is the place. The matte canonical workers are roles, never narrators.
The fixed owner cabinet and gold tether signify external owner authority.
The shutter is the receiver's visible STOP. The job's cream ticket holds
chronological green/red/green receipt marks and remains after replacement.

Colors come from the existing World: cream #f3ead9, ink #3d3428, sage Wren,
blue/terracotta Juniper, gold authority and restrained red refusal. Typesetting
uses Noto Serif Display for the name and Noto Sans for clear supporting lines.
The name appears at the earned final reveal, rather than over every shot.

The sonic signature is D–A–E in soft resonant mallets, a physically final
mechanical latch, complete STOP silence, and an open interval resolving after
the new owner grant. The score and object sounds are original deterministic
synthesis; the narrative voice is disclosed local neural narration.

The receipt bridge preserves the real UI pixels and literal fields from the
verified signed receipt. Warm paper, restrained type and negative space link
miniature and artifact without fictional interface chrome.

Derived cuts reuse these source shots, voice takes, sound generators and colors.
Portrait framing favors the current physical actor. The new-grant shot retains
the full owner-to-worker connection on a narrow canvas, with one plain line.
