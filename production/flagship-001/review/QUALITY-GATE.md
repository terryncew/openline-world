# Advertising review — The Work Stays

This is the maker's visual/editorial review of the exported films and their
normal-speed browser playback. It is not independent audience research or a
claim of broadcast-level creative approval. Technical outcomes are recorded
separately in TECHNICAL-QA.json and PLAYBACK-QA.json.

**At five seconds.** A blank physical ticket has become someone's job. Wren is
arriving and the gold authority object enters from outside the worker. The film
opens with an object and a relationship, before naming the software. The opening
was edited to place Wren before the town establishing shot so this change occurs
within the first five seconds. Whether that earns a stranger's attention remains
an audience question; no cold viewer was available here.

**At thirty seconds.** Work has left a green mark on that same ticket. The owner
withdraws permission; the gold connection vanishes and the worker's ring turns
red. This is a reversal, with a physical shutter and complete silence to follow.
The room and job do not reset. The story has progressed before the proof UI.

**At the reveal.** Wren remains without authority. Juniper received a separate
owner grant; the same ticket now has green/red/green decisions and the bench
retains two work checkpoints. The final words attach the simple idea to OpenLine:
“The worker can change. Your authority doesn't.” They describe owner control and
the same permission bounds, not transfer of a revoked mandate. Nontechnical
comprehension has not been measured with an independent viewer.

**Developer desire.** The master ends with the possibility of building around
what stays. The developer ending follows with the actual repository and its
existing launch-preview.sh command. The buyer ending names owner grants,
exact-action receiver decisions and signed receipts within the demonstrated
local preview. Neither ending promises a live provider integration.

**Name and identity.** The final reveal holds the name alongside the idea. The
Square, canonical roles, fixed external owner, physical STOP, persistent ticket
and receipt-to-real-UI transition identify this film. Removing OpenLine would
also remove the specific demonstrated evidence and campaign objects; there is
no interchangeable montage of generic AI imagery or third-party logos.

**Reality bridge.** The red ticket mark cuts to the actual recorded ReceiptsPanel
with STOPPED / notes.read / MANDATE_REVOKED. At this point only Wren's two receipts
exist in the UI. Juniper's pre-grant failure has no signed receipt and no effect;
the film does not invent either. The complete three-receipt run is retained and
signature-checked. Note writing is a synthetic local checkpoint, not external
document editing or a deployed production system.

**Sound review limit.** The edit has physical object cues, a shared D–A–E motif,
and a measured interval of complete STOP silence. Narration is locally generated
neural speech, not a recorded human performer. All exported streams are decoded,
measured and played unmuted at normal speed. This agent has no audio perception:
naturalness, balance by ear and the final emotional effect remain unverified.

**Delivery judgment.** The seven cuts are a coherent, playable production with
one visual and sonic canon and retained factual provenance. The miniature remains
the repository's stylized geometry. This record does not assert photorealism,
independent advertising approval or fulfillment of a subjective Super Bowl
quality standard. Those claims would require actual human review.
