# Storyboard and animatic plan

The master is constructed from SHOTS.json's reusable footage and EDITS.json's
edit decisions. Each shot retains its classification in both manifests.
Review contact sheets and frame captures are the visual storyboard. The complete
playable master supplies the moving reference; no separate imagined animatic
is substituted for the delivered film.

1. **The object.** Close high three-quarter bench angle. The cream ticket
   occupies the center, its blank surface ready for an accountable history.
2. **The place.** Low, broad Square silhouette; warm architecture and
   controlled lateral movement. No transaction or backend fact in this scene.
3. **Useful worker.** Wren's sage body enters the bench composition. Gold
   arrives from outside it; the camera finds the owner source.
4. **First consequence.** Proposal approaches along the gate axis. A real
   ALLOWED decision precedes the checkpoint. The timber bench holds continuity.
5. **Withdrawal.** The same gold object goes gray. The room does not reset.
6. **Boundary.** Near-frontal shutter composition, decisive red physical mass,
   STOP from the recorded receiver state. Foley resolves into silence.
7. **Match into fact.** Push onto the ticket's red mark; cut to the actual
   receipt UI on a cream field. Visible reason: MANDATE_REVOKED.
8. **Replacement.** Blue Juniper occupies the opposite side of the same job.
   Its reach remains unproductive while unadmitted. No red receipt invented.
9. **New grant.** A wider view includes the fixed external owner and a new
   gold link. Separate worker mandate, same permission bounds.
10. **Continuation.** Same receiver permits Juniper's exact action. Close
    onto two work pieces and three receipt marks on the original ticket.
11. **Possibility.** Two worker identities, one persistent job and history.
    Withdraw from the Workshop to the Square, then reveal the name.

Portrait adaptation reuses the same underlying shot renders with authored
per-shot crops that favor the relevant worker, shutter or ticket. Receipt
proof and end typography are laid out for the narrow frame rather than cropped
from their horizontal layouts. The Square remains context, not economic proof.
