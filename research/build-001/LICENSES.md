# LICENSES.md — BUILD 001 licenses

## Entries

Every BUILD 001 entry must be open source. The team chooses the
license and states it in the entry. If no license is stated, the
entry is not admissible: unlicensed code cannot be reused in the
final 24 hours and cannot ship.

## The shared record

Contribution text on the receiver (patches, tests, reviews,
decisions) is part of the public board. Submitting is agreement that
the contribution text, the decision, and the attribution record stay
public and unaltered. Correction is authorized and non-destructive:
the original bytes, the decisions, and every receipt stay untouched.

## Reuse in the final 24 hours

Reuse across teams requires the other team's explicit permission,
recorded with the reuse declaration (`builds_on`). Permission covers
the stated license of the reused entry. An integration that ships
credits every source in its source and its release docs.

## Organizer materials

The practice clip, its transcript, the pipeline proof, and this
package are published for the event. The hidden final clip's hash is
public; its bytes stay private until scoring.
