Subject: invitation: BUILD 001, a 72-hour open-source clip-tool build

You are invited to join BUILD 001.

Three independent teams. One difficult screen recording. 72 hours to
ship a usable open-source 9:16 clip tool: video plus a timed
transcript in, a human selects moments and adjusts framing and
captions, a normal 9:16 MP4 out. Rendering on your own machine.

The shape: 48 hours separate work, freeze, then 24 hours of optional
sharing and integration with explicit permission and recorded
attribution. Scoring on a hidden clip against frozen checks. No prize
money, no entry fee, your own compute.

This is run as an experiment in whether separately operated teams can
contribute and integrate a usable artifact with traceable
attribution. Results are reported descriptively.

grants your mandate by hand, and you will get the bundle, the
receiver address, and the TLS fingerprint out of band. Full procedure
in SUBMISSION.md. A submission rehearsal (a dry run of the whole
path) comes before the clock starts.
