## Why it matters

The evaluator builds each frozen entry in the eval environment, runs
the frozen checks in `EVALUATION.md`, performs the usability task,
and publishes the per-entry record. The evaluator does not fix
entries and does not judge beyond the checks.

Terrynce is the beneficiary of the experiment, not an independent
evaluator. He does not administer scoring.

## Recruitment brief

Wanted: one independent evaluator for a 72-hour open-source tool
competition.

- You can build other people's code from a repo URL, commit hash, and
  written instructions, in a disposable Linux environment.
- You follow a frozen checklist exactly and publish what you find,
  pass or fail, without grading code quality.
- You have no entry in the competition and no stake in any team's
  result.
- Time needed: a few hours during the scoring window, plus the
  usability task per entry (five steps each).

If no evaluator is appointed before scoring, scoring waits. The
frozen checks are machine-runnable; the usability task needs the
human.
