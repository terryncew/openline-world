# PREVIEW.md — BUILD 001, the short version

## What this is

BUILD 001 is a 72-hour event. Three independent teams get the same
hard screen recording and build an open-source tool that turns
horizontal video into vertical clips.

The tool takes a video and a timed transcript. A person picks the
best moments, adjusts the framing and the captions, and exports a
normal vertical MP4. All rendering happens on each team's own
computer.

This is an experiment. The question is whether separate teams can
build something usable together with clear credit for who did what.
No prize money. No hype.

## The shape of the 72 hours

First 48 hours: each team works alone, then freezes its entry. Final
24 hours: teams may share and combine work, but only with explicit
permission, and every reuse is credited.

The clock starts only after three approved teams have joined and each
has done a practice submission. Scoring uses a hidden clip the teams
never see. No fixes allowed after scoring starts.

## The frozen rules

The scoring checks are frozen and public before anyone starts. The
export must be exactly 720 by 1280, with readable captions timed to
the transcript and nothing in the bottom protected strip. A short
usability test by someone who didn't build the tool is part of it.

Award rules are frozen too. Pass or fail, no ranking. If nothing
passes, the findings get published honestly anyway.

## The pipeline proof

We proved the task is doable with free tools. A 14-second horizontal
clip became a captioned vertical MP4 in 8 seconds on a small 2-CPU
machine, using 319 MB of memory. Every check passed, including caption
timing at 10 points across the clip.

One real finding: a common caption tool misrenders at full size, so
the proof uses a different method and documents the pitfall for
participants.

## The clips

Teams practice on a public clip from the agent-economy film. They are
scored on a different hidden clip. Only the hidden clip's fingerprint
is public. Same requirements for both.

## Who judges

The evaluator seat is vacant. The evaluator builds each entry,
runs the frozen checks, does the usability test, and publishes the
results. Appointing one is your call. Scoring waits until the seat is
filled. You are the beneficiary, not the judge.
