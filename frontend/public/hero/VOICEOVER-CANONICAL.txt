CANONICAL VOICEOVER — authority-continuity demo
Frozen 2026-10-05 by owner order. Do not edit, "improve", or paraphrase.
The strength is that it teaches one thing cleanly: replacement does not
equal inherited authority, and history survives the handoff.
Target: ~45-50 seconds at a normal calm speaking pace.

---

Here's a problem we're about to have with AI agents.

If one worker gets replaced, the new one shouldn't automatically inherit everything the old one was allowed to do.

In this demo, Wren has permission to work on a job. The receiver checks that authority before anything happens, and keeps the receipt.

Then Wren is revoked.

Wren can still try, but the receiver stops the action.

Juniper replaces Wren, and the job and its history are still there. But Juniper doesn't inherit Wren's permission.

The owner has to explicitly authorize Juniper before the work can continue.

The model can change. The authority and the history don't have to.
